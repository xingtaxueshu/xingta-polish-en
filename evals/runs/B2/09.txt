Two coders coded the interview transcripts independently using NVivo 12. In the first round, Cohen's kappa between the two coders was 0.71, which is regarded as substantial agreement (Landis & Koch, 1977). The disagreements were discussed until consensus was reached, and the codebook was revised accordingly. Finally, we obtained 43 codes, which were grouped into 7 themes. It should be noted that the coders are both the authors of this paper, so the inter-coder reliability may be over-estimated.
