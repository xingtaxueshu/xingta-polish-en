Increasing scholarly attention has been paid to adolescents' extensive reading. Many studies have shown that extensive reading is helpful to vocabulary growth (Nation, 2001; Krashen, 2004). Krashen (2004) argued that free voluntary reading is the most important factor in language acquisition, and he also pointed out that students who read more get higher scores. However, few studies have examined how reading habits change after students enter secondary school. In China, studies on this topic are still very rare. Therefore, this paper aims to fill this gap.
