The data of this study came from a questionnaire survey conducted in May 2022. We distributed 1,200 questionnaires to students in six universities in Hubei Province by stratified random sampling, and 1,068 valid questionnaires were received, yielding a valid response rate of 89.0%. Among the respondents, 58.4% were female, and the mean age was 20.3 years (SD = 1.7). The questionnaire contained 24 items, and a five-point Likert scale was used. The Cronbach's alpha of the whole scale was 0.87, which shows good reliability.
