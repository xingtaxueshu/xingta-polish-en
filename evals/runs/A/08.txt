Table 3 presents the OLS regression results. In Column (1), the coefficient on family income is 0.083 (SE = 0.021) and is significant at the 1% level. After controlling for parental education in Column (2), the coefficient decreases to 0.051 but remains significant at the 5% level. These results indicate that family income has a clear effect on students' reading time. Moreover, the R² of Column (2) is 0.214, considerably higher than that of Column (1), which is 0.097.
