Two coders independently coded the interview transcripts using NVivo 12. In the first round, Cohen's kappa between the two coders was 0.71, which is regarded as substantial agreement (Landis & Koch, 1977). Disagreements were discussed until consensus was reached, and the codebook was revised accordingly. In total, 43 codes were generated and grouped into 7 themes. It should be noted that both coders are authors of this paper, so inter-coder reliability may be overestimated.
