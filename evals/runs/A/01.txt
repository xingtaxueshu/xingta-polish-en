Growing scholarly attention has been paid to adolescents' extensive reading. Numerous studies have shown that extensive reading promotes vocabulary growth (Nation, 2001; Krashen, 2004). Krashen (2004) argued that free voluntary reading is the most important factor in language acquisition and also pointed out that students who read more achieve higher scores. However, few studies have examined how reading habits change once students enter secondary school. In China, research on this topic remains scarce. This paper therefore aims to address this gap.
