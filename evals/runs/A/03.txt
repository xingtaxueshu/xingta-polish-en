The concept of digital literacy has been defined by scholars from diverse perspectives. Some regard it as a set of technical skills, such as operating software and searching for information online. Others view it as a form of critical ability that involves evaluating the credibility of online information. In this paper, digital literacy is defined as the ability to find, evaluate, and create information using digital technology. This definition draws on Gilster (1997) but adds the element of "creation," because creating content has become an everyday activity among young people.
