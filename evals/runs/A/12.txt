This paper applies text-mining methods to analyze 312 English novels published between 1850 and 1900. Using topic modeling, we extracted 20 topics and found that the proportion of topics related to domestic life rose from 12.5% in the 1850s to 18.3% in the 1890s. The results show that domestic themes gradually became a central focus of novels in this period. This finding offers new evidence for literary historians seeking to understand the Victorian novel. However, the corpus includes only novels available in digital archives, so its representativeness requires further examination.
