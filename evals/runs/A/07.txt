To estimate the effect of the reading program, we employ a difference-in-differences design that compares the change in reading scores in treated schools with that in control schools before and after 2019. The coefficient on the interaction term is 3.42 (p < 0.01), indicating that the program increased students' reading scores by 3.42 points, or about 0.15 standard deviations. The results are robust to controlling for school-level covariates, and the parallel trends assumption is supported by the event-study figure (Figure 2). However, causal interpretation should be treated with caution because the program was not randomly assigned.
