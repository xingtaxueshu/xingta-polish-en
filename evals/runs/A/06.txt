The data for this study were drawn from a questionnaire survey conducted in May 2022. We distributed 1,200 questionnaires to students at six universities in Hubei Province using stratified random sampling and received 1,068 valid responses, yielding a valid response rate of 89.0%. Of the respondents, 58.4% were female, and the mean age was 20.3 years (SD = 1.7). The questionnaire contained 24 items rated on a five-point Likert scale. The Cronbach's alpha for the full scale was 0.87, indicating good reliability.
