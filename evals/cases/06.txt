The data of this study came from a questionnaire survey conducted in May 2022. We distributed 1,200 questionnaires to students in six universities in Hubei Province by stratified random sampling, and 1,068 valid questionnaires was received, the valid rate is 89.0%. Among the respondents, 58.4% were female, the mean age was 20.3 years old (SD = 1.7). The questionnaire contains 24 items, and a five-point Likert scale was used. The Cronbach's alpha of the whole scale is 0.87, which show a good reliability.
