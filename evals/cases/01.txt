With the development of society, more and more scholars pay attention to the extensive reading of adolescents. Many researches have shown that extensive reading is helpful to vocabulary growth (Nation, 2001; Krashen, 2004). Krashen (2004) argued that free voluntary reading is the most important factor of language acquisition, and he also point out that students who reads more gets higher scores. However, few studies has examined how the reading habits changes after students entering secondary school. In China, the study on this topic is still very rare. Therefore, this paper aims to fill in this gap.
