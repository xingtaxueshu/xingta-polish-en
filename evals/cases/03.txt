The concept of digital literacy has been defined by different scholars from different perspective. Some scholars regard it as a set of technical skill, such as operating the software and searching the information online. Others think it is a kind of critical ability, which include evaluating the credibility of online information. In this paper, digital literacy is defined as the ability to find, evaluate, and create information by using digital technology. This definition is borrowed from Gilster (1997), but we add the element of "create" on it. The reason why we do this is because that creating content have become an everyday activity of young people.
