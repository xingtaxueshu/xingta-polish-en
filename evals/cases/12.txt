This paper use text mining method to analyze 312 English novels published between 1850 and 1900. Based on topic modeling, 20 topics are extracted, and we find that the proportion of the topics related to domestic life has increased from 12.5% in the 1850s to 18.3% in the 1890s. The result shows that the domestic theme gradually becomes the focus of the novels in the period. This finding provides a new evidence for the literary historians to understand the Victorian novel. However, the corpus only includes novels in the digital archive, so the representativeness need further examination.
