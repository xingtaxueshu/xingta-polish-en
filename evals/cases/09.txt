Two coders coded the interview transcripts independently by using NVivo 12. In the first round, the Cohen's kappa between two coders was 0.71, which is regarded as substantial agreement (Landis & Koch, 1977). The disagreements was discussed until the consensus was reached, and the codebook were revised accordingly. Finally, we got 43 codes, which are grouped into 7 themes. It should be noted that the coders are both the authors of this paper, so the inter-coder reliability may be over-estimated.
